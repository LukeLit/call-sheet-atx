# FAQ page

`/faq`, data in `src/data/faq.ts`. Plain answers for working artists and gig workers.

Covers: what Open Call is; where to start; part-time and uneven work (HAAM takes part- or full-time musicians, teachers and DJs; TWC helps people with uneven work); that we can't say if someone is eligible; **people in tech, games or design who are out of work** (some programs may fit, some won't: TWC isn't only for artists, arts programs define who counts, HAAM is for musicians); why only Austin (a demo); how current the information is; who to tell about a mistake; who is behind it.

It also carries Luke's story: he came to New York as a starving artist and leaned on programs when illustration work got sparse, then moved through web design, print, and now games and apps, and people across tech are feeling a similar squeeze as the job market shrinks.

Policy: never promise eligibility. Keep every claim to what the programs database holds.
